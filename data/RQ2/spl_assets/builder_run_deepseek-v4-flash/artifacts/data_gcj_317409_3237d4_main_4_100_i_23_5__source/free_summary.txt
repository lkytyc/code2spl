This code solves a problem where a person starts at a given position and moves according to a sequence of directional commands (N, S, E, W). The goal is to determine the earliest time the person can meet another individual who starts at a different location and moves in the four cardinal directions. It uses BFS to find the nearest point on the person's path from the other individual's starting position, then checks whether the meeting can occur by comparing distances and timing along the path, outputting the meeting time or "IMPOSSIBLE" for each test case.
