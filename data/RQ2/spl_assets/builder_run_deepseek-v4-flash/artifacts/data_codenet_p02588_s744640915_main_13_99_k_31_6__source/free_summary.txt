The code reads a series of decimal numbers as strings, converts each to an integer by removing the decimal point and scaling to a common denominator of 10^9, then filters out those that are not multiples of 100,000. It computes the divisors of 100,000,000 and uses them to count pairs of valid numbers whose product, when scaled, is a multiple of 100,000,000, outputting the total count of such pairs.
