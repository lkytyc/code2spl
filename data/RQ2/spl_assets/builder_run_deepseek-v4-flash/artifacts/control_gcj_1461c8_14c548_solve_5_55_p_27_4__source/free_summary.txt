This code implements an interactive algorithm that communicates with a judge or external system through standard input/output. It performs a series of queries and responses in multiple phases: first, it sends 17 queries with value 100, then 68 queries with varying values based on modulo arithmetic, followed by three rounds of queries to collect and analyze data, and finally sends additional queries to complete the interaction. The algorithm tracks and compares numerical results to determine an index and uses that information to make subsequent decisions in the protocol.
