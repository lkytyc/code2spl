This code processes a series of queries to maintain a dynamic set of numbers and answer median-related queries. It uses two priority queues (a max-heap for the lower half and a min-heap for the upper half) to keep track of the median, along with accumulated sums to compute the sum of absolute differences from the median. For each query, it either inserts a new number with an associated cost or outputs the current median and the total cost of making all numbers equal to that median.
