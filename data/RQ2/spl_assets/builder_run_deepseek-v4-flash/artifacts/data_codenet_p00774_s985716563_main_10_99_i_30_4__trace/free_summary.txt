This code implements a game scoring and board-clearing simulation. It reads a series of test cases, each with a 5-column board of numbers. For each case, it repeatedly scans columns for matching runs of 3, 4, or 5 equal non-zero values, awards points based on the match length and value, removes those matched cells, and shifts remaining cells down to fill gaps. The process repeats for 11 rounds, then outputs the total points for that case.
