`AreaCalculator` is a class that groups five geometric area calculations under a single radius value.

You instantiate it with a `radius`, which is stored and reused across all methods except one. The methods work as follows:

- `calculate_circle_area` — returns the area of a flat circle using that radius.
- `calculate_sphere_area` — returns the total surface area of a sphere with that radius.
- `calculate_cylinder_area` — takes a `height` parameter and returns the total surface area of a closed cylinder (both caps plus the lateral face).
- `calculate_sector_area` — takes an `angle` in radians and returns the area of a circular sector (pie-slice portion of a circle).
- `calculate_annulus_area` — takes `inner_radius` and `outer_radius` parameters and returns the area of a ring (annulus) between two concentric circles. Notably, the instance's own `self.radius` is not used here; the result depends entirely on the two arguments.

All calculations use `math.pi`. There is no input validation, no unit enforcement, and no handling of nonsensical inputs like negative radii. The class holds no state beyond the initial `radius`.
