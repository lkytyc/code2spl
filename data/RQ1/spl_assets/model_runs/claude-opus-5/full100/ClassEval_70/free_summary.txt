`PersonRequest` is a simple data-validation class for creating a person record. It takes three string inputs on construction — a name, a sex, and a phone number — and validates each one immediately, storing either the valid value or `None` if validation fails.

Validation rules:
- **name**: must be non-empty and at most 33 characters long.
- **sex**: must be exactly one of three string values — `"Man"`, `"Woman"`, or `"UGM"`.
- **phoneNumber**: must be non-empty, exactly 11 characters, and composed entirely of digits.

There's no error raised on bad input; the field just silently becomes `None`. The class has no methods beyond the constructor and its three private validators, and it does no further processing or output — it's purely a holder for validated field values.
