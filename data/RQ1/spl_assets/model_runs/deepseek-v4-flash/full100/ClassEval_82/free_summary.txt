The `StockPortfolioTracker` class manages a collection of stock holdings alongside a cash balance. It is initialized with a starting cash amount and an empty portfolio. Stocks are represented as dictionaries containing `name`, `quantity`, and `price`. The class supports adding or removing stock quantities by name, buying and selling stocks with cash balance validation, and computing portfolio value. Buying a stock only succeeds if sufficient cash is available, then deducts the cost and increases the held quantity (merging with any existing position of the same name). Selling a stock only succeeds if enough of that stock is held, then removes the quantity and adds the proceeds to cash. The portfolio’s total value is calculated as cash plus the sum of each holding’s price-times-quantity. A summary method returns both the total portfolio value and a list of holdings with their individual market values.
