The code defines an `RPGCharacter` class representing a game character with attributes such as name, hit points, attack power, defense, level, and experience. It supports attacking another character by reducing their HP based on the attacker’s attack power minus the defender’s defense, with a minimum damage of 1. It can heal itself by restoring 10 HP, capped at 100. Experience gain is handled through `gain_exp`, which accumulates experience and triggers level-ups when the experience threshold for the current level is reached; remaining experience is carried over. A level-up increases the character’s level (up to 100), resets experience, and boosts HP, attack power, and defense. The `is_alive` method checks whether the character still has positive HP. The class primarily manages character state, combat interactions, progression, and survival status.
