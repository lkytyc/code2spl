The `WeatherSystem` class manages weather data for a specific city. It is initialized with a city name and stores temperature, weather condition, and a weather data dictionary. The main `query` method accepts a dictionary mapping city names to weather details—including temperature, weather condition, and temperature units—and optionally a requested temperature unit (Celsius or Fahrenheit). If the city is not present in the provided dictionary, it returns `False`; otherwise, it updates the internal temperature and weather fields and returns a tuple containing the temperature (converted to the requested unit if necessary) and the weather condition. The class also provides a method to change the target city and separate methods for converting between Celsius and Fahrenheit.
