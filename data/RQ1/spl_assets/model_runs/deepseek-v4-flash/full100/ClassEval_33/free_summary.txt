The `DiscountStrategy` class computes the total cost of a customer's cart and applies an optional discount promotion. It is initialized with a `customer`, a `cart` (a list of items with `quantity`, `price`, and `product` keys), and an optional `promotion` function. The `total()` method calculates the sum of `quantity * price` for all cart items and stores it privately. The `due()` method returns the final amount owed by subtracting the promotion discount from the total; if no promotion is set, no discount is applied. The class provides three static promotion functions: `FidelityPromo` gives 5% off if the customer's fidelity score is at least 1000; `BulkItemPromo` deducts 10% per item for any line with quantity 20 or more; and `LargeOrderPromo` gives 7% off if the cart contains at least 10 distinct products. Each promotion takes an order-like object and returns a discount amount.
