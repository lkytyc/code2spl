The `TriCalculator` class implements basic trigonometric functions for angles given in degrees. It includes a `cos(x)` method that approximates the cosine using a Taylor/Maclaurin series expanded to 50 terms and returns the result rounded to 10 decimal places. To support that, the class provides a `factorial(a)` method that computes the factorial of a positive integer and a `taylor(x, n)` method that converts degrees to radians and evaluates the alternating cosine series. The `sin(x)` method also converts degrees to radians and computes sine using a Taylor series, adding terms until they become smaller than approximately `1e-15`, then rounding to 10 decimal places. The `tan(x)` method returns sine divided by cosine when cosine is nonzero; otherwise it returns `False`. The methods interact so that `tan` relies on `sin` and `cos`, while `cos` relies on `taylor` and `factorial`.
