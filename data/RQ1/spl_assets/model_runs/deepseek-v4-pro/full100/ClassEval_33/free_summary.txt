This code defines a `DiscountStrategy` class that calculates the total cost of a shopping cart and applies promotional discounts.

**Inputs:**
- `customer`: A dictionary containing customer information, including a `fidelity` score.
- `cart`: A list of items, where each item is a dictionary with `quantity`, `price`, and `product` keys.
- `promotion`: An optional callable (function) that takes the `DiscountStrategy` instance and returns a discount amount.

**Behavior:**
- The `total()` method sums the cost of all items (quantity × price) and stores it internally.
- The `due()` method calculates the final amount owed by subtracting any applicable discount from the total. If no promotion is provided, no discount is applied.
- Three static promotion methods are provided:
  - `FidelityPromo`: Gives a 5% discount if the customer's fidelity score is at least 1000.
  - `BulkItemPromo`: Gives a 10% discount on items where the quantity is 20 or more.
  - `LargeOrderPromo`: Gives a 7% discount if the cart contains at least 10 distinct products.

**Outputs:**
- `total()` returns the total cart value.
- `due()` returns the final amount after discount.
- Each promotion method returns a discount amount (a number).
