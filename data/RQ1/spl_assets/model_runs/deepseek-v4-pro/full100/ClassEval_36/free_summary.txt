This code defines an `EmailClient` class that simulates a basic email inbox with storage capacity limits.

**Inputs:**
- Each client is created with an email address (`addr`) and a storage capacity (`capacity`).
- Emails are sent with a recipient, content, and size.

**Key behaviors:**
- **Sending email:** When one client sends an email to another, it checks if the recipient has enough remaining capacity to store the email. If yes, the email is added to the recipient's inbox with a timestamp and marked as "unread." If not, the sender's own inbox is cleared (oldest emails first) to free space, and the send fails.
- **Fetching email:** A client can fetch one unread email at a time from its inbox. Fetching marks that email as "read" and returns it. If no unread emails exist, it returns `None`.
- **Capacity tracking:** The client tracks total occupied size by summing the sizes of all emails in its inbox.
- **Clearing inbox:** When triggered, the client deletes oldest emails from the front of the inbox until enough space is freed or the inbox is empty.

**Outputs:**
- `send_to` returns `True` if the email was delivered, or `False` if the recipient lacked space.
- `fetch` returns an email dictionary (with sender, receiver, content, size, timestamp, and state) or `None`.

**Important interactions:**
- The sender's inbox is cleared when a send fails due to the recipient being full—this is the only time `clear_inbox` is called.
- The `is_full_with_one_more_email` method is used by the sender to check the recipient's capacity before sending.
