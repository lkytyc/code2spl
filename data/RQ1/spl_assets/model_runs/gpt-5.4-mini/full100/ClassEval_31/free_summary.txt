This code defines a class, `DataStatistics4`, that provides four static utility methods for basic statistical calculations on numeric data.

- `correlation_coefficient(data1, data2)`: Takes two equal-length sequences of numbers and computes their Pearson correlation coefficient. It calculates each sequence’s mean, then uses the covariance-style numerator and the product of the two standard-deviation terms as the denominator. If the denominator is zero, it returns `0` instead of dividing by zero.

- `skewness(data)`: Takes one sequence of numbers and computes a skewness measure. It finds the mean, variance, and standard deviation, then uses the third central moment with a formula that depends on the sample size. If the standard deviation is zero, it returns `0`.

- `kurtosis(data)`: Takes one sequence of numbers and computes excess kurtosis. It finds the mean and standard deviation, returns `math.nan` if all values are identical, otherwise computes the fourth central moment and subtracts `3` to produce excess kurtosis.

- `pdf(data, mu, sigma)`: Takes a sequence of numbers and parameters `mu` and `sigma`, and returns a list of values from the normal distribution probability density function evaluated at each data point.

All methods are static, so they are called on the class itself and do not depend on instance state. The only imported module is `math`, used for square roots, exponentials, π, and `nan`.
