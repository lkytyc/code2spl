`AreaCalculator` is a class that groups several geometric area formulas under a single `radius` value set at construction time.

**Input:** A `radius` passed to `__init__`, stored as an instance attribute. Individual methods take additional arguments where needed.

**Methods and what they compute:**

- `calculate_circle_area` — area of a flat circle (πr²), no extra arguments.
- `calculate_sphere_area` — surface area of a sphere (4πr²), no extra arguments.
- `calculate_cylinder_area(height)` — total surface area of a closed cylinder (2πr(r + h)), taking `height` as a parameter.
- `calculate_sector_area(angle)` — area of a circular sector given an angle in radians (r²θ/2), taking `angle` as a parameter.
- `calculate_annulus_area(inner_radius, outer_radius)` — area of a ring (annulus) between two concentric circles (π(R² − r²)), taking both radii as parameters. Notably, the instance's `self.radius` is not used here; the method is fully determined by its two arguments.

**Output:** Each method returns a single `float` representing the computed area.

**Interactions:** The methods are independent — none calls another. The class uses Python's `math.pi` for precision. There is no validation of inputs, so negative or zero radii would silently produce mathematically consistent but geometrically meaningless results.
