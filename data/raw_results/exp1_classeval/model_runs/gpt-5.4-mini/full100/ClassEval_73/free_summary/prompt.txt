  Returns `True` if `hp` is greater than `0`, otherwise `False`.

The class supports basic combat, healing, experience gain, and leveling. Methods interact through shared character state: `attack` changes another character’s `hp`, `gain_exp` may call `level_up`, and `level_up` improves the character’s stats.
