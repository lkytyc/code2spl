- `get_upcoming_events(num_events)` returns up to `num_events` events whose `start_time` is at or after the current time. It scans the stored events in their existing order and stops once it has collected the requested number.

Important interactions:

- `get_available_slots` depends on `is_available` to test each generated one-hour slot.
- All methods operate directly on the shared `events` list, so the results depend on the order and contents of events previously added.
