`MinesweeperGame` represents a single game session. It takes two constructor arguments: `n` (the grid size) and `k` (the number of mines to place).

There's a bug in mine placement worth flagging: the random coordinates are not checked for collisions, so two mines can overwrite the same cell, meaning the actual mine count can be less than `k`. One notable issue: `sweep` does not implement flood-fill, so revealing a cell with a `0` (no adjacent mines) does not automatically open neighboring blank cells the way classic Minesweeper does.
